Version 2.2.1

EMPAD2

  • Opening an EMPAD2 RAW file no longer defaults to flipping rows. That is an EMPAD convention and not one EMPAD2 shares, so every EMPAD2 scan came in upside down until you noticed and undid it. Which detector wrote the file follows from its length, so the right default is chosen before the panel appears. EMPAD files are unchanged, and anything you tick yourself still wins.

The RAW scan dimensions panel

  • The detector transforms are no longer hidden behind a disclosure triangle, and sit above the calibrations. The orientation is the one setting here that is wrong by default as often as it is right, and getting it wrong is not obvious afterwards — it simply mirrors the scan. The calibrations below it can be left blank and filled in later; this cannot.
  • The panel is sized by its contents rather than fixed, so there is no longer a band of empty space above the buttons, and it grows and shrinks smoothly as the calibrations section opens and closes.
