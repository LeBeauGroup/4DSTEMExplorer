Version 2.3

Non-raster scans

  • Scans that were not acquired as a raster — spirals, constant-angular-velocity paths, and lattices that span the field edge to edge — now open. Their recorded positions (from scan_positions.npz or the metadata sidecar) are used to place each frame, and off-grid paths are gridded onto a canvas with density compensation.
  • A RAW file's length is now checked against the scan path's frame count rather than the raster size, so these files are no longer rejected as the wrong size.
  • Scan positions that were never visited are left out of the scan image's histogram and automatic contrast, so empty corners no longer squash the useful range.
  • A microscope library and settings pane for the instrument-specific scan metadata.

Display

  • Right-click the diffraction pattern to choose its colour map (viridis, inferno, magma, plasma, turbo, twilight or gray).
  • A histogram below the pattern sets the black and white points of whichever panel you clicked last — pattern or scan image — with draggable handles and an Auto button.

The RAW scan dimensions panel

  • The sheet now follows the height of its contents, so notes that appear after the scan positions are read are no longer cut off.
