Version 2.0.2

Fixed

  • Annular detector radii no longer collide. Driving the inner radius past the outer one used to leave them equal — an annulus of zero width, collecting nothing. Each now pushes the other and a gap of at least one pixel is kept. The inner control could also never widen the annulus from the inside, because its slider stopped at the outer radius; it no longer does.
  • acBF "Zero Aberrations" now clears the sliders. It cleared the coefficient vector and then let the defocus and astigmatism controls write their old values straight back over it, so only the higher orders — which have no control of their own — actually cleared.
  • The pattern keeps its size. It was a second flexible element in the sidebar and grew with the window height, pushing the detector controls down. It is now a square as wide as the column, and the sidebar content is pinned to the top left with the spacing below the title bar tightened.

Aberrations in the calibration

  • The coefficients acBF refines can now be applied to the dataset's calibration and are written to the exported EMPAD metadata: C1 as defocus in metres, and the rest as an aberrations list carrying Krivanek (n, m), magnitude in ångström, in the detector frame.
  • The acBF aberration report now gives each term as a magnitude and an azimuth rather than a pair of Cartesian halves, in Krivanek C_{n,m} notation throughout so the labels and the numbers agree.
